# Financial Sales Performance Dashboard – Key Insights



#### Executive Summary



The company generated ₹2,50,84,41,014 (\~250cr) in revenue and ₹37,55,30,511 (\~37cr) in profit from 100000 orders across 100000 customers during the analysis period.



#### Key Insights

#### 

###### Revenue Performance

* Total Revenue: ₹2,50,84,41,014
* Highest Revenue Year: 2023
* Revenue Growth Trend: 0.07% for the year 2023



###### Product Insights

* Top Category: Sessional Fruits and Vegetables (\~₹42cr)
* Most Profitable Category: Household items (\~₹6.28cr)
* Best Selling Product: Mops, Household Items (\~₹1.63cr i.e. is \~26% of total profit generated by Household Items)



###### Customer Insights

* Top Customer Segment: Consumer
* Top 20% Customer Revenue Contribution: ₹9,00,28,635 i.e. \~35.88% of total revenue.



###### Regional Insights

* Best Performing Region: North (₹12,69,67,205)
* Best Performing State: Maharashtra (₹5,18,41,774)



###### Operational Insights

* Most Used Shipping Mode: First Class
* Highest Performing Outlet Type: Medium (Total Orders = 33497)
* Average Discount: 0.25%



###### Recommendations

* Focus marketing efforts on Seasonal Fruits \& Vegetables as it generates the highest revenue.
* Increase availability of Household Items since they contribute the highest profit.
* Analyze customer preferences in the Consumer segment to improve customer retention.
* Identify factors driving strong sales in the North region and apply similar strategies in other regions.
* Explore growth opportunities in Maharashtra, the highest-performing state.
* Monitor discount levels and their impact on profit to maintain healthy margins.
